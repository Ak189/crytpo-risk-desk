Important assumptions

For linear futures contracts, position notional = risk budget ÷ (stop distance + estimated costs). Notional is further limited by your margin cap and allowed leverage. This tool is educational, not investment advice or a guarantee of a maximum loss. A stop order can fill worse than expected or fail during extreme volatility; liquidation, funding, gaps, order-book depth, exchange minimums, and contract specifications are not fully modeled. Use isolated margin and check liquidation distance separately. Enter live market-cap and liquidity data manually; the app does not connect to an exchange or place orders. Settings are saved only in this browser and may disappear if its website data is cleared.
